  • Print flat side down, as-is. No supports needed.
  • PETG or ASA for outdoors; PLA works but can sag in hot sun.
  • 4 walls, 20–25% gyroid infill. Two fit on one plate.
  • Per base: one M6 hex nut + one M6×25 bolt or wing screw.
  • Stakes: 6″+ landscape staples (straddle the slot) or 7–9 mm tent stakes / spikes.
