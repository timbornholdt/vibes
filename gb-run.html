Before you buy
  • Pokémon and Zelda carts are heavily counterfeited. Check the label, the board, and the stamped number on the cart front.
  • Old carts with save batteries can die; Gold, Silver and Crystal especially (their clock battery too). Ask the seller if the save is tested, or budget for a battery swap.
  • “Works on both” (black) carts run on an original Game Boy. GBC-only (clear) carts don't.
